# Welcome to the AI Summarization Handbook

A beginner-friendly guide to summarizing articles, meeting notes, documents, and emails with **free AI** — cloud free tiers, local LLMs you run on your own computer, or anything in between.

Written in plain English, step by step, never condescending. Like a friend showing you how it works.

Created by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC**, founded by Dylan Asdale — a registered nurse turned AI author whose book *The Massive AI Manual* and hub site dylanasdale.github.io explain AI “like a friend would.”

## Why summarize with AI?

Summaries help you:

- Catch up on a long article in seconds
- Turn messy meeting notes into clear action items
- Get the gist of a contract, policy, or report before reading it in full
- Triaging your inbox by pulling out decisions and next steps

AI is good at summarizing. The key is doing it **safely** and **knowing which tool to use for what.** This handbook walks you through both.
