Local vs cloud — which to use when

Both free cloud tools and local LLMs can summarize well. The choice is about privacy, convenience, and your computer.

Cloud free tiers

Pros:

  • No install, works in a browser or app
  • Usually the most capable models
  • Handles very long documents
  • Works on any device, including phone

Cons:

  • Your text goes to the provider’s servers
  • Usage limits (messages per day)
  • Requires internet
  • Less control over data retention

Best for: Everyday articles, newsletters, general research, quick inbox triage

Local LLMs (Ollama, LM Studio, AnythingLLM)

Pros:

  • 100 % free, forever
  • Offline — works without internet
  • Nothing leaves your computer
  • Best for sensitive or private text

Cons:

  • Must download and install software
  • First model download is large (2–8 GB)
  • Needs a reasonably modern computer (8–16 GB RAM recommended)
  • Smaller free models may be less capable than top cloud models

Best for: Meeting notes with client names, HR documents, invoices, health information, anything you would not post publicly

Quick decision guide

If you… Use
Want zero setup Cloud free tier
Summarize sensitive work text Local LLM
Have spotty internet Local LLM
Need to summarize a 100-page report Cloud (larger context window)
Are on a phone or tablet Cloud free tier
Want total privacy, no account Local LLM

You can use both

Many people do: cloud for everyday articles and newsletters, local for anything private or work-related. Pick the tool that matches the sensitivity of the text.
